When the country burns

Before August

The grass still holds water and the nights are cool. Fires creep, stay low and leave patches, and many are lit on purpose to break up the fuel.

After July

The grass has cured and the wind is up. The same spark now runs for days, burns hot enough to kill canopy, and takes fences and stock with it.

Three rules it follows

These are what make the scores above mean something in a real fire season.

It never sees the year it predicts

Fire history comes only from earlier years. Two tests check this, one by scrambling a year's answers and confirming nothing the model reads moves.

It stops reading on 30 April

The fire season runs May to October. August weather would describe a fire, not forecast it, so the model can be run in May.

The month is approximate

Satellites revisit the same ground every few weeks, so a fire is dated to the window it was seen in. Close to 31 July that dating blurs.
