How this year was used

When does the Territory burn?

Every fire on record, sorted by the month it was detected. The tall bars late in the year are the damaging fires; the bump in May and June is deliberate burning done while the country is still damp.

If a place burns, will it burn again?

Each bar answers one question: of all the places that burned in some year, what share burned again exactly that many years later. The flat line is the finding. Waiting does not make a place safer.

What happens the year after?

Pick a row for what happened this year, then read across for what happened next year. Each row adds to 100.

Can one place burn twice in a year?

It burns once in the cool part of the season and again after the country dries out.

Does burning early stop a worse fire later?

Rangers across northern Australia light cool fires before August so there is less grass left to carry a hot one in September. Each bar is the chance of a fire after July next year.

We tested it by pretending to be in the past

Showing a model the answers and then asking it the same questions proves nothing. So we cut the record at a year, let the model learn only what came before, and asked it about years it had never seen. Then we moved the cut forward and did it again.

Five models, two baselines

What the forest leans on

Weather in ember, fire history in slate.

It never sees the year it is guessing

Everything the model knows about fire history comes from years before the one it is asked about. Two tests check it, including one that scrambles a year's answers to confirm nothing the model reads moves with them.

It only uses weather known by 30 April

The fire season runs May to October. Using August weather would be describing a fire rather than predicting it, so the model stops reading at the end of the wet season and can be run in May.

The month is approximate

Satellites check the same ground every few weeks, so a fire is dated to the period it was spotted in. Close to the end of July that dating blurs, which limits how sharp the before and after split can be.
